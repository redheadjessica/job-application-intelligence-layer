# Anecdote Bank — your true stories, indexed for reuse

Personal stories and lived details for cover letters and application answers. **This bank (plus the
application canon and you directly) is the ONLY permitted source of personal stories** — the
writer never invents, embellishes, or composites. Each entry is tagged with its source.

**Usage rules for the writer:**
- Check **Used in** before reusing: never send the same story to the same company twice **within the
  last 12 months**, and vary stories across a company's multiple roles.
- **⭐ The no-reuse rule has a time horizon — it is not permanent.** A story sent to a company a year
  ago is not spent. Beyond 12 months, reuse is fine: re-angle it for the new role rather than avoiding
  it. **Never trade the strongest available framing for novelty against a stale `Used in` entry.** The
  real failure this prevents: a letter came back deliberately weakened because the two strongest
  anecdotes were marked used-at-that-company eleven months earlier, so the writer fell back to a bland
  factual line — and it did so *silently*, with the reason buried in the review packet, so the letter
  simply read thin. A guardrail that degrades the deliverable to satisfy itself is mis-scoped.
- Match on **Themes / Use when**, not surface keywords. One or two sentences of story is the dose.
- Stories may be lightly re-angled for the role but never changed factually.
- The reconcile pass appends new entries and updates **Used in** — keep its formatting.

Entry format: heading = slug · Status/source · The story · Themes · Use when · Used in · Guardrails.
